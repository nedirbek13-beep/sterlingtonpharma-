The company

A UK-registered pharmaceutical wholesaler, built around documentation.

Sterlington Injectable Pharmaceutical UK Ltd sources sterile injectable and oral medicines from GMP-certified manufacturers and supplies hospitals, licensed distributors and institutional buyers. We do not sell to the public.

What we do

Precision sourcing for institutions that cannot accept uncertainty.

Hospitals, distributors and procurement teams need to know exactly what they are receiving, where it was made and that its documentation will stand up to inspection. That is the service Sterlington is organised to provide.

Our portfolio covers three dosage forms, solutions for infusion, powders for infusion and oral suspensions, presented under the SterliX product range. Every product is listed with its generic name, strength, dosage form and pack size, and supplied with batch-specific documentation.

We work with licensed and qualified partners only. Each relationship begins with a documented qualification step and continues with the same standard on every order.
