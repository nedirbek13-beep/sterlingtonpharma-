Who we supply

Four kinds of organisation, one documentation standard.

Licensed distributors

You receive full product documentation, batch records and regulatory support from day one. Our distributors are part of how we deliver quality to patients, and we treat them as long-term allies.

Hospital procurement

Hospital teams depend on reliability. We provide consistent lead times, full traceability and complete documentation with every order, prepared for inspection.

Pharmacy groups and clinics

Licensed pharmacy and clinic groups are supplied through qualified distribution channels, with the same batch documentation as hospital supply.

Public procurement and tenders

Product specifications and documentation are presented in a consistent, verifiable format suited to tender submissions and institutional purchasing.

Partnership process

From first conversation to active supply.

  1. 01

    Initial enquiry

    You contact us by form, email or phone. We respond within one business day with an introduction and a product list relevant to your market.

  2. 02

    Qualification

    We verify your licensing, distribution infrastructure and regulatory standing. This protects both parties and ensures compliant supply.

  3. 03

    Product selection

    Together we identify the products most relevant to your market, taking therapeutic need and regulatory clearance into account.

  4. 04

    Documentation package

    We provide batch records, GMP certificates and Certificates of Analysis for your local regulatory submission.

  5. 05

    First shipment

    Cold-chain logistics are arranged and tracked from the manufacturer to your warehouse, with follow-up after delivery.
