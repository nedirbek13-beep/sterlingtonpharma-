Product information

The product information on this website is provided for healthcare professionals, pharmacists, hospital procurement teams, licensed distributors and other organisations qualified to purchase medicines. It describes the products Sterlington is able to supply to such organisations. It is not medical advice, it is not an advertisement to the public and it is not an offer to supply medicines to any person or organisation that is not appropriately licensed.

Product listings give the generic name, strength, dosage form and pack size of each product. They do not contain dosing instructions, indications for use or safety information. Prescribing and administration decisions must be based on the approved product information for the destination market, which is supplied with product documentation.

Availability of any product depends on its registration status in the destination market and on completion of partner qualification. Pack images are shown for identification and may differ from the presentation supplied in a given market.
