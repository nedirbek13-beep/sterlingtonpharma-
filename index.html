Quality process

Six controlled steps between the manufacturer and your warehouse.

We source exclusively from GMP-certified manufacturers and document every order from qualification to delivery. Read the full quality process.

  1. 01

    Supplier qualification

    Only manufacturers holding current GMP certification are considered, and each completes due diligence before a product is listed.

  2. 02

    Product verification

    Specifications, certificates and batch documentation are checked against the order before release.

  3. 03

    Documentation package

    Certificates of Analysis, the manufacturer's GMP certificate and batch records are compiled for your local regulatory submission.

  4. 04

    Cold-chain logistics

    Temperature-controlled transport from the manufacturer to your warehouse, tracked throughout the route.

  5. 05

    Traceability

    Full batch traceability on every order, so any product can be followed from source to destination.

  6. 06

    Post-delivery follow-up

    We confirm receipt and condition after delivery and remain available for documentation queries.

The company
